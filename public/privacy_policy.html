Overview

Yaazh Cabs ("Yaazh Cabs", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Yaazh Admin mobile application. Yaazh Admin is intended for authorized administrators and staff to manage bookings, customers, drivers, vehicles, payments, invoices, employees, reports, and notifications.

By using Yaazh Admin, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Access to the application is intended for authorized business users and is not intended for children.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled through Yaazh Admin may be used to:

  • Authenticate and manage authorized admin and staff accounts.
  • Manage customer bookings and travel information.
  • Manage driver and vehicle records.
  • View and manage payment-related business information.
  • Create and manage invoices.
  • Manage employee information.
  • Generate and view business reports.
  • Deliver relevant booking and business notifications.
  • Maintain and operate the application and related business services.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our systems.

3. App Permissions

Camera, Photos, and Files

Yaazh Admin may request access to the camera, photo gallery, or files when necessary for authorized business functions such as uploading driver documents, vehicle documents, or profile photos.

These permissions are used only when the corresponding feature requires them. The app does not use device location services.

4. Data Sharing

Yaazh Cabs does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by Yaazh Cabs and its authorized systems solely as necessary to operate Yaazh Admin and provide its business functions. We do not intentionally disclose information to unrelated third parties.

5. Storage and Data Retention

Backend and Database

Yaazh Admin uses a Node.js backend and MySQL database to support the application's business operations.

Retention

Business records and information associated with accounts, customers, bookings, drivers, vehicles, payments, invoices, and employees may be retained for the lifetime of the relevant business records, unless deletion is requested and applicable retention requirements or legitimate business needs require otherwise.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information handled through Yaazh Admin against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their login credentials and should not share passwords or account access with unauthorized persons.

7. Notifications

Yaazh Admin may receive push notifications related to bookings and business operations. Notifications are used to help authorized users stay informed about relevant booking updates.

The application does not use notifications for unrelated advertising.
